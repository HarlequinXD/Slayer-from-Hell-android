# Slayer from Hell (Android)

A first-person shooter test scene in the style of Quake/Doom, written from scratch in Java with OpenGL ES 3.0.

* **Retro resolution, point-sampled**: the scene renders at 270 lines tall (the same as RPG Pocket Editor's default; width follows the screen's shape) into an offscreen buffer that is scaled up to the screen with nearest-neighbour sampling. Every texture is point-filtered (nearest texel, nearest mip level).
* **PBR rendering**: GGX/Smith/Schlick shading of albedo, normal, height, ambient-occlusion, roughness, metalness and emission maps, ACES tone mapping, fog.
* **Parallax occlusion mapping**: an adaptive ray march through each material's height map with binary-search refinement, plus soft self-shadows toward the nearest lights.
* **Procedural materials**: every texture is generated at start-up (hell brick, riveted metal panels, cracked floor tiles, diamond plate, concrete, crates, light panels, carved pillars, flowing lava, gunmetal, walnut, leather, fabric, brass, shell hulls).
* **Doom-style sector maps** (`SectorMap`, `Level`): shared vertices, sectors as closed loops with floor and ceiling heights, textures and a special (lava, exit, secret, flickering light), and placed things (demons, health orbs, shells, lamps, the player start). Sectors can sit inside sectors: pillars, pits, raised daises and platforms. Walls, steps and lintels come from the lines between sectors; floors and ceilings are ear-clipped with their holes cut out. Collision, floor heights, rays, a 0.5 m navigation raster and baked light visibility all come from the sectors.
* **Doom rules**: demons have the zombieman's stats (20 health, pain chance 200/256, ~0.3 s to aim, a 3-15 damage shot, no splash). Her plasma does 5-40 per bolt (Doom's plasma rifle, no head-shot bonus), the chainsaw bites for 2-20 about nine times a second, lava hurts ~20 a second. Skill levels as in Doom (`Skill`): every placed thing has easy / normal / hard flags; I'm Too Young To Die and Hey, Not Too Rough use the easy set (the first halves damage taken and doubles ammo), Hurt Me Plenty the normal set, Ultra-Violence and Nightmare! the hard set (Nightmare: faster demons and fireballs, double ammo, demons come back after 12 s). The menu asks for the skill before each level; the editor's Entities panel sets each thing's flags. Campaign demons are spread out, with none in sight of the start (3 per map on the easy skills, 6-7 on Hurt Me Plenty, 8-11 on Ultra-Violence).
* **Main menu**: Campaign (Hell's Gate, The Molten Keep), Custom levels (play, edit, delete), Level editor, Settings (look speed, invert look, render resolution, frame rate), Quit; pause and level-complete screens in game. Reaching an exit sector ends the level.
* **Level editor** (after RPG Pocket Editor's sector editor): an on-screen cursor that snaps to the grid (1, 0.5 or 2 m), moved with the d-pad (hold to repeat) and acted on with Apply.
  * **Sectors** have three tool pages in the inspector. Draw: draw a polygon corner by corner, cut a sector with a line, rectangle, stairs, close, undo point, cancel, undo/redo, validate, clean up, grid. Edit: select, move vertex/edge/sector, split edge, extrude, duplicate, delete, special, pillar, player start. Surface: floor and ceiling up and down, floor/ceiling/wall textures, lava, exact heights.
  * **Entities**: the list of entity types on the left; place, select, move, rotate and delete with the cursor.
  * **3D View** (after RPG Pocket Editor's): the map in the game's own renderer, demons posed at their spawns. Texture browser on the left; drag the left half to move (floating joystick) and the right half to look; tap a floor, ceiling or wall to select it (outlined in ember) and double-tap to paint it. Walls can be painted face by face (overrides saved in the map, kept when a wall is split). Fly or walk with collision, Up/Down while flying, Raise/Lower a selected floor or ceiling, back to the player start, Play here, undo/redo.
  * The editor uses the menu's hellfire theme: charred panels, ember accents and glowing titles.
  * Every change is validated (no crossing walls, no partial overlaps; a sector is either beside another or wholly inside it), undoable, and saved automatically. Play tests the level straight from the editor.
* **Campaign maps** (`Maps`) are built through the editor's own validated operations: chamfered halls, twin pillars, a stepped dais and altar, lava trenches, a window and doorway into the armoury, stairs to a gallery, an octagonal courtyard with an island altar ringed by lava pools and bridges, terraces, side wings, a pillared temple, exit shrines.
* **Quake-style controller**: ground friction/acceleration, air control (strafe-jumping works), jumping, stepping up stairs, head bob, landing dip, recoil kick.
* **Double-barrel shotgun, baked from high poly**: `tools/bake/GunBaker.java` sculpts a 5.5-million-triangle side-by-side shotgun (hollow bores and chambers, serrated rib, brass bead, case-hardened action with scroll engraving, slotted screws, hinge pin, top lever, safety, triggers and guard, chequered walnut, ribbed butt pad) and bakes it onto a 744-triangle game mesh (UV-unwrapped into 79 charts on a 512² atlas, 1.7 mm per texel): normal, height, ambient occlusion, albedo, roughness and metalness maps (`app/src/main/assets`). Rebuild with `javac tools/bake/GunBaker.java && java -Xmx4g -cp tools/bake GunBaker app/src/main/assets /tmp/preview [mesh]`.
* **Third person, playing as the warden**: an over-the-right-shoulder camera that tightens when she aims and pulls in rather than clipping through walls. She is a rigid-segment rig over the baked low poly (`hero.bin` holds one mesh per body part, 2,274 triangles without the glaive) with procedural, velocity-driven animation (`Hero.java`): each foot plants during stance and arcs through the swing, with stride and cadence scaled by speed and a walk-to-run blend, heel-strike and toe-off roll, and two-bone IK for the legs; the pelvis bobs, shifts, rolls and twists; the torso counter-rotates, leans into the run and bends with the aim; jumps tuck the legs and landings compress the body; the braid swings on a spring. A light push on the stick walks, a full push runs. The double barrel replaces her glaive: stock in the shoulder, both hands placed by arm IK on the grip and forend (the left hand fetches shells during the break-open reload). She carries it at low ready and turns toward where she is going; when shooting or reloading she squares up to the camera. Pellets leave the muzzle toward whatever the crosshair is on.
* **Locomotion (in the spirit of GTA V)**: start / cycle / stop / pivot behaviour driven by her real velocity.
  * Feet plant without sliding, with stride matched to speed and heel strike and toe off.
  * Digitigrade legs: full thighs, the knee forward, thick calves back to the hock, then a slim segmented cannon and a cloven hoof. Three-bone IK: the hoof is planted, the cannon hangs from it, and two-bone IK solves hip to hock.
  * Foot IK finds the floor under each hoof (and just ahead of it, so the hoof tip clears step edges), so on stairs each hoof lands on its own step and the pelvis drops to let the lower leg reach.
  * She leans into starts, rocks back on stops and banks into turns. Turning on the spot makes her shuffle her feet round.
  * Walking into a wall makes her recoil. Pushing against a wall, she puts her free hand on it.
* **Chainsaw arm, baked from high poly**: `tools/bake/ChainsawBaker.java` sculpts a forearm-mounted chainsaw she slides her right arm into (padded opening, grip bar inside, riveted bands, leather straps and buckles; a two-stroke engine with fuel tank, finned cylinder, head cover, plug and lead, muffler and twin pipes, recoil starter and air filter; front housing with brass nuts, chain-brake guard on its hinge, bucking spikes; a 53 cm guide bar; and a shell caddy). The paint is amber hazard chevrons over bare-metal chips and grime. Every link of the 136-link chain is modelled. The low poly is box modelled at 902 triangles (818 body + 84 chain loop): the major forms are geometry and everything small goes to the bake (normal, height, AO, albedo, roughness, metalness on a 512² atlas at 2.6 mm per texel). The chain bakes onto a tiling 8-link strip (`chain_*.png`), so the chain loop scrolls its texture to run. Rebuild with `javac tools/bake/ChainsawBaker.java && java -Xmx4g -cp tools/bake ChainsawBaker app/src/main/assets /tmp/preview [low|high]`.
* **Hand cannon on her left forearm, chainsaw on her right**: the warden wears a green hand cannon (`CannonBaker green`: army-green paint, green plasma core and vents). FIRE raises it along the aim and throws green plasma bolts (30 damage, 1.8x on head shots, a little splash) at whatever the crosshair is on; energy cells are the ammo (pickups give 15). The double barrel below is no longer used. Previously: **Shotgun in one hand, chainsaw on the other**: hold SAW to raise the chainsaw and rev it (the chain speeds up, the engine note climbs, exhaust puffs from the pipes). Where the bar touches a wall it throws sparks and leaves scars. FIRE shoots the double barrel one-handed from the hip, with the stock under her left arm. The reload is one-handed: she flicks the gun open, the empties fly, and she brings her saw arm across and pushes the open chambers down onto the two spare shells standing in the caddy on its sleeve, then flicks it shut.
* **The warden, baked from high poly**: `tools/bake/HeroBaker.java` sculpts a part-based figure (3.3 million triangles when meshed at 1.5 mm): charcoal undersuit, army-green painted steel plates with panel grooves, rivets, soot, scratches and edges chipped to bare steel, dark steel trim, a crested helmet with a glowing amber visor slit, a long braid, leather belt, pouches and gauntlets, a crimson sash, and a glaive whose obsidian blade glows through molten cracks. Each part's low poly is lofted through rings fitted to the sculpt by ray casting (2,516 triangles with the glaive, 49 UV charts, 512² atlas at 4.6 mm per texel) and baked to normal, height, AO, albedo, roughness, metalness and emission maps. Rebuild with `javac tools/bake/HeroBaker.java && java -Xmx6g -cp tools/bake HeroBaker app/src/main/assets /tmp/preview [low|prev|high|obj]`.
* **Break-action**: fire the right then the left barrel (heavy kick, 9 buckshot pellets each), break it open on the hinge, the extractors throw the fired cases over your shoulder, the left hand loads fresh shells and snaps it shut. Bullet holes, sparks, dust and splinters, muzzle flash light and smoke.
* **Demons**: red-armoured, horned, on the same hoofed legs and procedural locomotion as the warden (each one is a Hero rig driven by its own controller), with orange eye slits and a hand cannon on the right forearm that throws blue fireballs. `HeroBaker ... demon` bakes the demon (2,752 triangles, 512² maps) from the same sculpt with a demon variant; `tools/bake/CannonBaker.java` bakes the hand cannon (434 triangles: armoured sleeve, cooling rings, glowing vents, caged plasma core and conduits, slotted muzzle).
  * **AI framework** (`Ai.java`): a reusable state machine with a blackboard (alerted, can see, last known position, distance, cooldowns). Perception is sight (range, field of view, line-of-sight ray every 0.15 s), hearing (gunshots within 32 m of path distance) and damage.
  * **States** (`Demon.java`): idle, patrol, alert (roar), chase, attack (charge and fire), strafe, pain (stagger) and dead (topple).
  * **Navigation** (`Nav.java`): a Dijkstra flow field over the grid toward the warden that respects stairs, drops, headroom and lava; demons go straight at her when the way is clear.
  * **Combat** (`Enemies.java`): only two demons may attack at once (attack tokens). Fireballs lead their target a little, hurt on contact (12) and splash (7), and light the scene blue. Demons have 150 health: shotgun pellets do up to 11 each with range falloff and double on head shots, and the chainsaw chews through them. Dying resets the keep.
* **Sound**: all effects are synthesised in code.

Controls: left side = move stick, right side = drag to look (also while holding Fire), FIRE / RELOAD / JUMP buttons (hold Jump to bunny-hop).

The APK is built by GitHub Actions (Actions → latest run → artifact `SlayerFromHell-debug`).


## Tests

Unit tests run in CI (`gradle testDebugUnitTest`). `SectorTest`: nested sectors triangulate with their holes, line sides, collision and heights, rays, the editor rejects bad shapes and allows nesting, undo/redo and save round trip, the line tool, stairs, extrude/duplicate, every campaign map is valid with reachable demons, pickups and exit, and the map store. `LocomotionTest`: steady idle, walking covers ground, and climbing the stairs keeps each hoof on its step without the pelvis popping. `AiTest`: demons idle until they see or hear her, then chase and shoot; no more than two attack at once; nobody walks into lava; every spawn can reach her; shots kill them and corpses stay put; fireballs hurt.
